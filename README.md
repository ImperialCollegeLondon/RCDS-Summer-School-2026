# RCDS Summer School 2026

## Preparation

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you are a member of staff or postgraduate who performs research at Imperial you should [sign up for Imperial's Copilot Business plan](imperial_copilot_business_instructions.md). If you are not one of these types of user, you should sign up to [GitHub Education](https://github.com/education). Each of these processes may have delays so **you should start whichever process is relevant to you at least 10 days before the summer school**. If you have problems, contact a course organiser.
